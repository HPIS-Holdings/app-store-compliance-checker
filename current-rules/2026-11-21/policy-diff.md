# Changes since October 10, 2026

The November 21 catalog rechecks the excerpts for all 102 existing rules and adds 11 rules. Quotes were matched against official text after normalizing whitespace and document markup. Apple’s DocC Markdown supplied the text for its JavaScript documentation pages. Expandable Google Help sections were included. Dates have day precision. The October snapshot retains its original hashes and dates.

## Apple

- The October 16 review guidelines expand 1.2 to cover AI content creation, move hidden features to 2.5.19, add 2.3.14 for creative assets, clarify permitted external-purchase destinations in 3.1.1(a), and clarify removal and alternative-distribution consequences under section 5. [Guideline changes](https://developer.apple.com/news/?id=92th7e5i)
- App updates and age questionnaire edits require Shared Location and Dating and Relationships declarations from November 16. All apps must complete them by January 12, 2027. Dating and Relationships carries a minimum 18+ rating. Brazil has separate descriptors and an official-rating process. [App Store Connect announcement](https://developer.apple.com/news/?id=yxkh08us)
- The Speech framework requires a purpose string even for on-device recognition. [Speech permission](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/bundleresources/information-property-list/nsspeechrecognitionusagedescription)

## Google Play

- The consolidated policy used here is the October 29 edition. It includes revised wording for account deletion. The current regional developer-verification threshold is Android 8 (API 26). External content links participants now have until February 1, 2027 to report successful US downloads and pay the related fees. [Developer Program Policy](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/18265346?hl=en) · [Developer verification](https://developer.android.com/developer-verification) · [US program terms](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/15582165?hl=en)
- Google’s billing-library version 7 extension has expired. The regular submission deadline for version 8 is August 31, 2027. [Billing Library deprecation FAQ](https://developer.android.com/google/play/billing/deprecation-faq)

## Scope

The review covers the catalog’s quoted provisions and the changes listed above. Storefront-specific contracts, unpublished Play Console answers and an app’s actual runtime behavior require separate evidence. The [October exclusions](../2026-10-10/DEPRECATED.md) and [community notes](../2026-10-10/community-notes.jsonl) retain their original dates.
